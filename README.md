# Mindsight Journey – Train Vision Without Eyes

[![Mindsight Journey](https://static.mindsightjourney.com/images/og-home.webp)](https://mindsightjourney.com)

**[Mindsight Journey](https://mindsightjourney.com)** is a multi-platform **eyeless vision training app** for developing inner sight through **guided meditation**, **brainwave entrainment** and **blindfolded perception training**, on **PC, Mac, iOS, Android and VR (Meta Quest)**.

Mindsight is the trained human capacity to perceive colors, shapes and images without using the eyes, documented by researchers since the 1920s. Mindsight Journey guides you through 18+ interactive modules, from cardiac coherence breathing and Hemi-Sync® meditation to blindfolded color, shape, number, letter and picture perception.

## Interactive modules

A progressive sequence of exercises to develop your inner perception ([full descriptions](https://mindsightjourney.com)):

1. **Cardiac Coherence Breathing** – the starting point: a guided pacer to breathe at 0.1 Hz (about six breaths per minute) for a calm, focused state.
2. **Meditation** – guided meditation with Hemi-Sync® binaural audio, promoting synchronization between the brain's hemispheres.
3. **Colored Words** – a Stroop-effect warm-up: name the ink color, not the word, to shift from analytical reading to direct perception.
4. **Simple Colors** – sense which color is shown while blindfolded; the app tracks your accuracy over time.
5. **Simple Shapes** – identify circles, triangles, squares and other basic shapes while blindfolded.
6. **Colored Shapes** – identify both the shape and its color.
7. **Numbers** – single digits, which demand finer inner perception.
8. **Letters** – uppercase letters alternating black-on-white and white-on-black to provoke visual impressions.
9. **Colored Pictures** – full-color photographs and detailed images for experienced practitioners.
10. **Blinking Shapes – Alpha** – brainwave entrainment at 10 Hz (alpha).
11. **Blinking Shapes – Theta** – 6 Hz (theta), associated with dreamlike awareness and inner imagery.
12. **Blinking Shapes – Gamma** – 40 Hz (gamma), associated with peak concentration and clarity.
13. **Mind Projection Fractals** – immersive fractal visualizations with full 360° VR support.
14. **Field of Pinholes** – a field of light points to train peripheral awareness.
15. **ESP Card Game** – test your extrasensory perception with the classic Zener card symbols.
16. **Cheesecloth Effect** – VR-exclusive mesh-like overlay at the boundary between physical and inner sight.
17. **Staring White** – VR-exclusive bright white field for sustained gazing.

## Platforms and controls

- **PC (Windows & Mac)** – optimal visuals with full keyboard control: `ESC` menu, `CTRL` music, `→` next, `SPACE` voice commands.
- **VR (Meta Quest)** – immersive 360° experience: Menu button navigation, `Y` options, `A` select.
- **Mobile (iOS & Android)** – top button menu, tap to select, long press for options.

### Voice commands & accessibility

Navigate through modules and adjust settings with **voice commands** for a hands-free experience, on all platforms (PC, VR and mobile). Designed for blind users and anyone who prefers hands-free navigation while blindfolded.
